A shop drawing is a fabrication-level drawing produced by (or for) the contractor, fabricator, or installer — it shows exact dimensions, materials, hardware, and assembly details needed to actually build or install something like millwork, casework, or tile and stone work. Construction documents, by contrast, are the architect's design-intent drawings used for permitting and bidding.

Shop drawings translate that design intent into build-ready instructions for the shop floor and job site, which is where our "shop-floor logic" approach comes in.
